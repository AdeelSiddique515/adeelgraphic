09 — FAQ

Before you ask

How do we start?

Send the short form below, an email, or an Instagram DM. Tell me what your business is and what you need designed. I'll reply with a clear scope, timeline and a fixed quote — you approve it, and only then does work begin.

How much does it cost?

Every project is quoted individually, because a logo, a menu and a 30-ad campaign are very different jobs. Message me for a quote — it's specific to your project, and there's no obligation to go ahead.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope: a logo or a small set of posts is days, a full identity or campaign is longer. Your quote always includes a delivery timeline, and I work to it.

What do you need from me?

Just your business basics: what you sell, who it's for, any existing logo or photos you have, and examples of styles you like (or hate). After that, feedback at the concept stage — I'll handle everything else.

Can you match my existing brand?

Yes — I can design inside an existing identity, refresh one that's drifting, or build a new one from scratch. Send what you have and I'll tell you honestly which route makes sense.

Where can I see more of your work?

Start with the work on this page — the design projects shown in full, the 23 videos, the five live wedding websites and the 30-design Christmas campaign show the range: logos, kits, booklets, social sets and ad designs. If a style you see matches what you want, mention it in your message and we'll start from it.
